## Full description (max 4000)
PakBazar is Pakistan's free marketplace to buy and sell almost anything — cars, property, mobile phones, electronics, furniture, fashion, jobs, services and more. Post your ad in under 2 minutes and reach buyers across the country.

WHY PAKBAZAR
• 100% free to post ads
• Buy & sell across 280+ cities in Pakistan
• All prices in Pakistani Rupees (Rs)
• Chat with buyers and sellers in real time
• Call or WhatsApp sellers directly
• Save your favourite ads and searches

EXPLORE EVERYTHING
Motors • Property • Mobiles & Tablets • Electronics • Home & Furniture • Men & Women Essentials • Kids • Jobs • Services • Pets • Sports & Hobbies • Business & Industrial — all in one app.

POST AN AD IN MINUTES
Add multiple photos, set your price, choose your city or attach your exact GPS location, and publish. Boost your ad to "Featured" to get more views and sell faster.

SMART & SAFE
• Powerful search and filters by city, price and condition
• Verified sellers and 5-star ratings & reviews
• Saved-search alerts notify you when a matching new ad is posted
• Mark items as Sold when you're done
• Report suspicious ads to keep the marketplace safe
• Built-in safety tips for in-person meetups

Whether you're upgrading your phone, selling your car, renting an apartment, or hunting for a great deal, PakBazar connects you with real buyers and sellers near you.

Download PakBazar and start buying and selling today — Pakistan ka apna bazaar, ab aapke phone par!
