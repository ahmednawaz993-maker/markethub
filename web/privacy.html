PakBazar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

PakBazar ("we", "us") is a classifieds marketplace that lets people in Pakistan buy and sell items. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using PakBazar you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address (for email sign-in) or an anonymous guest identifier. New email accounts may be asked to verify.
  • Listings you post: title, description, price, category, city/area, contact phone number, and photos you upload.
  • Location: if you tap "Use my current location", we capture your device's GPS coordinates and attach them to that listing. This is optional and only happens when you choose it.
  • Messages: chat messages you send to other users.
  • Favorites & saved searches: ads you save and search criteria you choose to save for alerts.
  • Device & notifications: a push notification token, and basic technical data needed to run the app.
  • Identity verification (optional): if you choose to get the ID-Verified badge, you upload a selfie and a photo of your CNIC. These images are stored privately and are visible only to you and our review team — never shown publicly. They are used solely to confirm your identity and keep the marketplace safe, and you can request their deletion.

How we use it

  • To show your ads to buyers and let buyers contact you.
  • To power search, favorites, saved-search alerts, and chat.
  • To send push notifications (new messages, matching new ads).
  • To keep the marketplace safe and respond to reports of abuse.

Sharing

Details you put in a listing (title, price, location, contact number, photos, seller name) are public to other users — that is the purpose of a classifieds listing. We do not sell your personal data.

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging) to operate the service. Their handling of data is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Data retention & deletion

Your ads and account data remain until you delete them or request account deletion. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

Children

PakBazar is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Delete any of your ads at any time from "My Ads".
  • Decline or revoke location and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data via the contact email.

Contact

Questions or data requests: support@pakbazar.pk

PakBazar is operated as an independent marketplace app. This policy may be updated; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
