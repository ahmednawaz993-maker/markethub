Delete your PakBazar account

You can request deletion of your PakBazar account and associated data at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the PakBazar app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap “Delete my account”.
  3. Confirm. Your request is sent to our team and processed promptly.

Option 2 — By email

Email ahmednawaz993@gmail.com from the email address on your account (or include your registered phone number), with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify and delete your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and account login.
  • Your ads/listings and their photos.
  • Your saved searches, favourites, and related records.
  • Your identity/address verification documents (selfie, CNIC, proof).

What may be retained

We may retain limited records required by law or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud (for example, transaction/escrow records), and content already shared with other users in completed deals, for a limited period. These are kept only as long as necessary and are then deleted.

Questions about deletion or your data: ahmednawaz993@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
